Who We Are

BUILT BY PEOPLE WHO KNOW THE ROAD

Starling Dispatch was founded on one simple idea: the carrier and the dispatcher should always want the same thing — maximum revenue per mile, with zero friction getting there.

Our Philosophy

A DISPATCH DESK FOR INDEPENDENT FLEETS

Most dispatch services treat owner-operators as a volume number — book a cheap load, move to the next call. Starling Dispatch was built differently. We maintain a focused ratio of trucks per dispatcher so every carrier gets real, dedicated attention: rate negotiation, lane planning, and a dispatcher who actually understands your equipment and home-time goals.

We work on a transparent percentage commission (5% to 7%). We only get paid when you get paid. That single structural rule shapes everything we do — from which brokers we approve, to how aggressively we fight for rate increases on your rate confirmations.

Today, Starling Dispatch supports Dry Van, Reefer, Flatbed, Step Deck, Power-Only, Hotshot, and Box Truck operators running lanes across the continental United States around the clock.

Carrier-First Loyalty

Every decision is filtered through one objective: does this put more gross revenue into the carrier's bank account?

Radical Commission Transparency

You inspect the original rate confirmation directly from the broker. No hidden spreads, no surprise deductions.

24/7 Road & Desk Support

Freight doesn't stop for business hours, and neither do we. Live dispatchers are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
