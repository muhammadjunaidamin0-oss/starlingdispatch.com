Who We Are

BUILT BY PEOPLE WHO KNOW THE ROAD

Starling Dispatch was founded on one simple idea: the carrier and the dispatcher should always want the same thing — more revenue per mile, with less friction getting there.

Our Story

A DISPATCH DESK FOR THE INDEPENDENT FLEET

Most dispatch services treat owner-operators as a volume number — book the load, move to the next call. Starling Dispatch was built differently. We work a focused book of carriers so every truck gets real attention: rate negotiation, lane planning, and a dispatcher who actually knows your equipment, your home-time needs, and your numbers.

We only get paid when you get paid. That single structural choice shapes everything else we do — from which brokers we'll work with, to how hard we push back on a lowball rate confirmation.

Today, Starling Dispatch supports dry van, reefer, flatbed, power-only, and box truck operators running lanes across the continental United States, with a dedicated team handling paperwork, factoring, and broker relationships around the clock.
