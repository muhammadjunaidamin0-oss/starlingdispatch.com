Common Questions

REFERRAL PROGRAM FAQ

When do I get paid?

Within 7 business days of your referral completing their first paid billing cycle after the 14-day free trial.

Is there a limit on how many people I can refer?

Absolutely not. Refer 1 or 100 — you earn $150 for each one who activates paid service. No cap, no fine print.

What if my referral signs up on their own without mentioning me?

Submit their details through the form before they sign up and we'll track the referral to you. First submission wins credit.

Do I need to be a Starling Dispatch client to refer?

No! Anyone can refer — existing clients, friends, family, trucking community members. If they activate, you earn.

What if my referral doesn't convert after the free trial?

The $150 is paid only upon paid activation. If they don't continue after the trial, no worries — there's no penalty to you either.
