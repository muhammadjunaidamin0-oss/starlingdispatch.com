Refund & Cancellation Policy

Simple, transparent, and fair — just like our service.

Last Updated: September 9, 2026

⚡ Quick Summary: We don't charge upfront fees — so there's nothing to refund. You only pay 6% commission on loads we successfully book for you. You can cancel any time, no questions asked.

1. Our Commission-Only Model

Starling Dispatch operates on a commission-only model. This means:

  • We charge no setup fees, no onboarding fees, and no monthly retainers.
  • Our 6% commission is only earned when we successfully book a load for you.
  • If we don't book you a load, you owe us nothing.

Because you never pay us upfront, there are no situations where a refund of a flat fee would apply. This policy covers commission disputes and trial period cancellations.

2. The 14-Day Free Trial

All new carriers begin with a 14-day free trial:

  • The free trial is completely free — $0.00 charge, no credit card required.
  • You receive full access to all standard dispatch services during the trial.
  • You can cancel before or at the end of the trial with zero obligation.
  • No automatic billing occurs at the end of the free trial — we will discuss continuation before any commission applies.

3. Commission Disputes

If you believe a commission was incorrectly calculated or charged in error, we encourage you to contact us within 7 business days of the load settlement. We will review the rate confirmation, broker settlement, and payment records and issue a correction or credit if an error is confirmed.

To file a commission dispute:

  • Email us at Support@starlingdispatch.com with the subject line: "Commission Dispute — [Your MC Number]"
  • Include the load reference number, broker name, and the amount in question.
  • We will respond within 3 business days.

4. Cancellation of Service

You may cancel your dispatch service arrangement with Starling Dispatch at any time. There are no cancellation fees or penalties. To cancel:

  • Email us at Support@starlingdispatch.com with your company name and MC number.
  • Or call our dispatch desk at +1 (916) 209-0883.

Upon cancellation, any loads already confirmed and in transit will remain under the original commission agreement until their completion. Loads not yet confirmed will be cancelled at no charge.

5. Non-Refundable Circumstances

While rare, there are specific circumstances where commission already earned is non-refundable:

  • Loads that have been successfully delivered and settled by the broker.
  • Commission withheld due to carrier misconduct, load abandonment, or FMCSA violations.
  • Loads where the carrier received full payment and we received our agreed commission.

6. Service Interruption

In the unlikely event that Starling Dispatch is unable to provide service due to technical or operational issues and you have suffered a measurable financial loss as a direct result, please contact us and we will review the situation on a case-by-case basis to determine appropriate remedies, which may include commission credits or service extensions.

7. Contact for Refund Inquiries

We pride ourselves on fair and transparent business practices. If you have any concerns about billing, commissions, or cancellation, please reach out directly:

  • Email: Support@starlingdispatch.com
  • Phone: +1 (916) 209-0883
  • Available: Monday – Sunday, 24/7
