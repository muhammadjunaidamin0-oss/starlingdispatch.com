Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Last Updated: September 9, 2026

1. Who We Are

Starling Dispatch ("we", "our", or "us") is a professional freight dispatching company operating in the United States. We provide load booking, rate negotiation, paperwork management, and dispatch coordination services for owner-operators and carrier fleets. Our website is located at starlingdispatch.com.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: Support@starlingdispatch.com or +1 (916) 209-0883.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information you voluntarily provide to us, and we limit that collection to what is strictly necessary to provide our dispatching services.

  • Contact Information: Company name, contact person name, phone number, and email address.
  • Business Information: MC (Motor Carrier) number, DOT number, equipment type (truck type), and preferred operating lanes or states.
  • Communications: Any messages or notes you include when submitting our carrier application form.

We do not collect payment card details, Social Security Numbers, driver's license numbers, or any other sensitive personal data through our website.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information you provide is used solely for the following purposes:

  • To contact you regarding your carrier onboarding application.
  • To set up and manage your dispatch account.
  • To book loads and negotiate rates on your behalf.
  • To send operational updates, load alerts, and service-related communications.
  • To respond to your inquiries or support requests.

We will never use your information to send unsolicited marketing emails or sell your data.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party. We only share your information in the following limited circumstances:

  • Load Boards & Brokers: We may share your MC/DOT number with licensed freight brokers and load boards (such as DAT iQ and Truckstop) as required to book loads on your behalf.
  • Service Providers: We use Google Workspace (Google Sheets, Google Forms) to store form submissions securely. Google's privacy policy applies to data processed through their services.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or government regulation.

5. Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses minimal, functional cookies to improve your browsing experience:

  • Preference Cookies: We store your cookie consent preference in your browser's localStorage so we do not repeatedly show you the consent banner.
  • Font & Resource Caching: Google Fonts may set third-party cookies for font loading optimization.

We do not use advertising cookies, analytics tracking pixels, or any form of behavioral tracking.

6. Data Retention

We retain your contact and business information for as long as you are an active dispatch client, plus a reasonable period afterwards for record-keeping purposes. If you request deletion of your data, we will remove your information from our active systems within 30 business days.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your state of residence, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of any inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information.
  • Opt out of any future communications from us.

To exercise any of these rights, please email us at Support@starlingdispatch.com with your full name and the nature of your request. We will respond within 30 business days.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended exclusively for business owners and operators aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

9. Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Form submissions are transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections and stored in access-controlled Google Workspace accounts.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of our website after such changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please reach out:

  • Phone: +1 (916) 209-0883
  • Website: starlingdispatch.com
