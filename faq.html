Common Questions

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything carriers ask us before signing up. Don't see your question? Reach out directly.

How does the 14-day free trial work?
You get full dispatcher support for 14 days with no credit card required and no commitment. If Starling Dispatch isn't the right fit, you can walk away at the end of the trial with nothing owed.
How much does Starling Dispatch charge?
We work on a transparent percentage or fixed commission basis, agreed with you up front. There are no hidden fees, no setup charges, and no surprise deductions — you see the rate confirmation and you see exactly what we take.
What equipment types do you dispatch?
We currently support dry van (53'), reefer, flatbed and step deck, power only, and box truck operations. Let us know your equipment type during setup and we'll match you with a dispatcher experienced in that freight.
What documents do I need to get started?
You'll need your active MC Authority, a current Certificate of Insurance, and a signed W-9. Our team reviews everything and completes any additional broker-specific paperwork on your behalf.
Do I have to take every load you find?
No. We present loads based on your stated lane preferences, equipment, and target rates, but you always have final say on what you haul. Your preferences can be updated at any time.
How quickly can I start hauling?
Most carriers are matched with a dispatcher and rolling on their first load within 24–48 hours of submitting authority, insurance, and W-9 documentation.
Do you handle factoring and invoicing?
Yes. We submit invoices the same day a load delivers and coordinate directly with your factoring company so your cashflow keeps moving without manual follow-up on your end.
How do I reach my dispatcher?
Every carrier is paired with a dedicated dispatcher reachable directly — not a rotating call center. Support is also available 24/7 for check calls, detentions, breakdowns, or rerouting.

Still have questions? Our team is happy to walk you through it.
