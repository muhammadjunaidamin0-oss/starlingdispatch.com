Carrier Answers

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about our dispatching service, transparent 6% commission, rate confirmations, and 14-day free trial.

How does the 14-day free trial work?

Our 14-day free trial gives you full access to a dedicated dispatcher with zero dispatch commission fees for two full weeks. There is no credit card required to sign up, no binding long-term contract, and no hidden catches. You keep 100% of your gross earnings during the trial period so you can evaluate our load negotiation and service risk-free.

Do I maintain control over which loads I accept?

Yes, 100%. You are the captain of your truck. Your assigned dispatcher negotiates and presents loads based on your predefined lane preferences, but no rate confirmation is signed without your explicit verbal or text approval. You never have to haul a load or region you don't want.

What is your dispatch fee structure after the free trial?

After your 14-day free trial, our standard dispatch fee is a flat 6% of gross load revenue (or 10% for dedicated 1-on-1 dispatcher service). We work on a transparent percentage basis, meaning we only make money when you make top dollar. You see the original Rate Confirmation directly from the broker every single time.

What documents do I need to start hauling with Starling Dispatch?

To onboard your carrier authority, we need 4 basic documents: (1) Copy of your active MC/DOT Authority Certificate, (2) Signed W-9 Form, (3) Certificate of Insurance ($1,000,000 Auto Liability & $100,000 Cargo), and (4) Notice of Assignment (NOA) if you use a factoring company.

How do billing and factoring work with your team?

As soon as you complete delivery and send us your signed Bill of Lading (BOL) or POD photos, our billing department immediately packages the rate confirmation, invoice, and BOL and submits them directly to your factoring company or broker quick-pay. You receive your funds without administrative delay.

What equipment types do you dispatch for?

We dispatch for 53' Dry Vans, Temperature-Controlled Reefers, Flatbeds, Step Decks, Power-Only units, Hotshot rigs (40' Gooseneck), and 26' Box Trucks across all 48 continental states.

What happens if I encounter detention or layover at a shipper?

Your dedicated dispatcher handles check-in tracking and immediately files for detention pay, layover compensation, or TONU (Truck Ordered Not Used) with the broker standard policy as soon as facility delays exceed free time. We fight for every dollar you are owed.

Is there any long-term contract or forced dispatch?

No. We operate strictly on a month-to-month, carrier-first agreement with zero forced dispatch. You can pause or stop using our service at any time with no penalties.
