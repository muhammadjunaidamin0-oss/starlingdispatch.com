Fast Onboarding Pipeline

FROM SIGNUP TO YOUR FIRST PREMIUM HAUL

Six steps, most carriers are rolling within 24–48 hours of submitting their authority.

01

Submit Authority

Send over your active MC Authority, copy of your Insurance Certificate, and signed W-9 format.

Takes most carriers under 10 minutes.

02

Dispatcher Match

We pair you with a dedicated dispatcher who knows your equipment type and preferred regions.

No call centers — one dispatcher, one relationship.

03

Set Preferences

Define your lane expectations, preferred equipment types, home-time schedules, and target rates.

Preferences can be updated any time as your needs change.

04

Broker Vetting

Every broker on your potential loads is checked for payment history before we present you an offer.

You only see loads that have already passed our screen.

05

Roll Out

Our dedicated dispatcher secures your first premium haul immediately. We dispatch, you drive.

Rate confirmations are reviewed with you before you commit.

06

Invoice & Get Paid

We submit your invoice and handle factoring coordination the same day your load delivers.

Cashflow keeps moving, load after load.
