Carrier Journey

THE 5-STAGE DISPATCH LIFECYCLE

01

Submit Authority & Documents

Send your active MC/DOT Certificate, W-9, and Certificate of Insurance ($100k Cargo / $1M Liability). We set up carrier packets with top brokers right away.

02

Define Lane Preferences

Tell your assigned dispatcher your target rate-per-mile, preferred operating states, maximum cargo weight, equipment specs, and home-time goals.

03

Rate Negotiation & Con Review

Your dispatcher aggressively negotiates with brokers on DAT/Truckstop spot boards. You receive and approve the official broker Rate Con before we confirm.

04

Dispatch, Pickup & Delivery

We send all loading details, facility contacts, and route instructions directly to your phone. 24/7 check-in support handles detentions or delay claims.

05

Same-Day Billing & Factoring

Once you send us the signed Bill of Lading (BOL), we instantly package the paperwork and submit it to your factoring company or quick-pay provider.

06

14-Day Free Guarantee

Enjoy 14 days of zero dispatch fees. Experience our service, evaluate your gross earnings boost, and decide if you want to continue!
